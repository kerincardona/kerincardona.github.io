Structured, data-driven approximation of linear dynamical systems. The method combines regularization and sparsity in the parameter estimation, with a focus on low-rank approximation. It does not depend on a particular system representation and does not assume an input-output partition of the data.

Related code: [SpLra](https://github.com/kerincardona/SpLra).

I am a PhD researcher in the Building Energy and Environment Group (BEE Group) at [CIMNE](https://www.cimne.com/) in Lleida, Spain, and a doctoral candidate at the Universitat de Lleida.

My work is about urban heat at neighbourhood scale, where weather stations are sparse and climate models are too coarse. I build and evaluate machine-learning models that turn coarse or incomplete data into high-resolution temperature fields, and I check how far those predictions can be trusted.

Research topics
======
- **Spatiotemporal downscaling of urban air temperature** with two-dimensional state-space models (Mamba-type backbones), benchmarked against recurrent and convolutional baselines over several random seeds.
- **Uncertainty quantification** for downscaling models with post-hoc conformal prediction (calibrated prediction intervals, coverage under seasonal and inter-annual shift).
- **Satellite land surface temperature and heat exposure** in Barcelona: Landsat-derived surface urban heat island intensity by Local Climate Zone (2013-2024), and multi-sensor benchmarks for thermal downscaling.
- **Learned surrogates for unsteady flows**: long-horizon autoregressive stability of neural models on vortex-dominated wakes.

Earlier work covered numerical linear algebra and data-driven system identification (sparsity, regularization and low-rank approximation).

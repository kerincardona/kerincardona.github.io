Code
======
- [vmamba2-urban-downscaling](https://github.com/kerincardona/vmamba2-urban-downscaling): spatiotemporal downscaling of urban air temperature with two-dimensional state-space models.
- [ecrs2026-barcelona-lst](https://github.com/kerincardona/ecrs2026-barcelona-lst): talk, posters, figure scripts and abstracts for ECRS 2026 (surface urban heat island by Local Climate Zone in Barcelona; multi-sensor LST benchmark).
- [lcz_tda_ftw](https://github.com/kerincardona/lcz_tda_ftw): topological data analysis of urban (LCZ) versus agricultural (FTW) shape morphology.
- [SpLra](https://github.com/kerincardona/SpLra): sparse, regularized, low-rank system identification.
